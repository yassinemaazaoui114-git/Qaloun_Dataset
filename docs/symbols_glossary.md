# Symbols Glossary — Qaloon Mushaf Al-Mu'allim

Resolved recitation-notation symbols. Check here before researching a symbol again. Source of each entry is given.

## Waqf (pause) signs — source: the printed legend "علامات الوقف" (printed page 15 of the front matter, scan page 618)

| Printed sign | Unicode in `text` | Meaning (as the mushaf's own legend states it) | First seen |
|---|---|---|---|
| م | ۘ U+06D8 | وقف لازم — a pause is required | not yet |
| ج | ۚ U+06DA | وقف جائز — pause or continue, both allowed | page 2, after الٓمٓ |
| صلى | ۖ U+06D6 | وقف جائز مع كون الوصل أولى — pause allowed, continuing is preferred | page 2, after رَّبِّهِمْ |
| قلى | ۗ U+06D7 | وقف جائز مع كون الوقف أولى — pause allowed, pausing is preferred | not yet |
| ∴ (three dots, appears twice) | ۛ U+06DB | علامة تعانق — mu'anaqa: if you pause at one of the two marked places you may not pause at the other | page 2, over رَيْبَ and فِيهِ |

Note: the legend's own example is the same ayah as page 2 line 10 (ذَٰلِكَ ٱلْكِتَٰبُ لَا رَيْبَ ۛ فِيهِ ۛ هُدًى لِّلْمُتَّقِينَ), which confirms the dot placement.

## Madd (elongation) — source: printed legend "المدود" (scan page 612)

The legend lists eight madd types: natural, muttasil, badal, lazim, munfasil, 'iwad, leen, 'arid. It explains them in words and shows the printed elongation sign next to each, but it does not map signs to Unicode code points. The madda signs on الٓمٓ (page 2, line 10) are transcribed with U+0653 (ٓ). Treat any other new elongation sign as unresolved and flag it in the review sheet.
